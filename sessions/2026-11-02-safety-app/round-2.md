The false positive cell is not evenly spread; the same neighborhoods will fill it.

## Lu

Arithmetic is a technical opacity question; Sumpter is right that it is unanswered, and wrong to think it is the whole case. 3. The burden shifts only if the flagged person can inspect the evidence behind the flag and the official, not the resident, must justify it.

CONFIDENCE: 4/5

MY CONDITIONS FOR YES: 1. A named accountable official per system, published. 2. Compelled tiered disclosure to auditors and to a flagged person's reviewer, under protective order if needed. 3. An appeal in which the institution carries the burden of justification.
